I'll answer this question about the standard count of continents.

The standard count of continents is **7 continents**. This is the most widely taught model in English-speaking countries and is used by many international organizations. The seven continents are:

1. Africa
2. Antarctica
3. Asia
4. Europe
5. North America
6. Oceania
7. South America

Note: Other models exist (such as the 6-continent model combining North and South America, or the 5-continent model), but the 7-continent model is the most commonly cited as "the standard count" in modern educational contexts.

FINAL ANSWER: 7
