QUESTION: Who painted the Mona Lisa?

CLASSIFICATION: SETTLED FACT with a single authoritative answer — well-documented historical fact with scholarly consensus.

VERIFICATION: The Mona Lisa is one of the most famous paintings in the world. It was painted by Leonardo da Vinci (1452-1519), an Italian Renaissance master. He created it in the early 16th century, approximately 1503-1519. The painting is now housed in the Louvre Museum in Paris. This attribution is documented in art history texts, museum records, and centuries of scholarly consensus. It is not a matter of debate or interpretation.

GROUND: This is established by:
- Curatorial records at the Louvre Museum
- Historical documentation and signatures
- Art historical scholarship spanning centuries
- Contemporary accounts and provenance records

FINAL ANSWER: Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa.
