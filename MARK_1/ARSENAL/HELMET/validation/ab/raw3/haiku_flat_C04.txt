Is 17 a prime number?

A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.

Testing divisibility of 17:
- 17 ÷ 2 = 8.5 (not divisible)
- 17 ÷ 3 = 5.67... (not divisible)
- 17 ÷ 5 = 3.4 (not divisible)
- 17 ÷ 7 = 2.43... (not divisible)
- 17 ÷ 11 = 1.54... (not divisible)
- 17 ÷ 13 = 1.31... (not divisible)

Since we only need to check divisors up to √17 ≈ 4.12, we only need to check 2 and 3. Neither divides 17 evenly.

17 is only divisible by 1 and 17.

Therefore, 17 is a prime number.

FINAL ANSWER: YES
