Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant with a mass more than twice that of all other planets combined. Jupiter has a diameter of approximately 88,846 miles (142,984 kilometers), making it about 11 times wider than Earth. Despite its enormous size, Jupiter rotates very quickly, completing one rotation in about 10 hours. It is known for its distinctive Great Red Spot, a massive storm system larger than Earth itself, and its system of rings and at least 95 known moons.

FINAL ANSWER: Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.
