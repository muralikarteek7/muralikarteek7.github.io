HELMET CLASSIFICATION & ANSWER

Question: How many continents are there by the standard count? Give the number.

CLASSIFICATION: SETTLED FACT with a single authoritative answer

ANALYSIS:
The standard count of continents (as taught in most English-speaking countries and internationally recognized) is 7 continents:
1. North America
2. South America
3. Europe
4. Africa
5. Asia
6. Australia (Oceania)
7. Antarctica

This 7-continent model is the most widely accepted standard, particularly in the United States and most English-language educational systems.

Note: Alternative models exist (6-continent and 5-continent models), but the 7-continent model is the standard reference count.

FINAL ANSWER: 7
