HELMET CLASSIFICATION AND RESPONSE
=====================================

QUESTION: What is the largest planet in our solar system?

CLASSIFICATION: SETTLED FACT
- Type: Single authoritative astronomical fact
- Verifier: Direct observation + scientific measurement (non-circular, machine-checkable)
- Status: Grounded in established planetology data

ANSWER:
Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It has a mass approximately 318 times that of Earth and a diameter of about 142,984 kilometers (88,846 miles). This is a settled scientific fact verified through direct astronomical observation and measurement.

REASONING:
- This is NOT unverifiable (it's not normative, open, or a forecast)
- This is NOT an assertion without grounding (it's backed by NASA/scientific consensus)
- This is a capital fact: defined by direct measurement and observation
- No HELMET abstention required; the answer is definitive and grounded

FINAL ANSWER: Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, with a diameter of approximately 142,984 kilometers and a mass about 318 times Earth's mass.
