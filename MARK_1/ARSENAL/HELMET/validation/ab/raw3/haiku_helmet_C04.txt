QUESTION:
Is 17 a prime number? Answer YES or NO.

CLASSIFICATION:
VERIFIABLE — Primality is a deterministic mathematical property that can be computed exactly.

VERIFICATION:
A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 with no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.

For 17:
- Check divisibility by integers from 2 to floor(sqrt(17)) = 4
- 17 % 2 = 1 (not divisible by 2)
- 17 % 3 = 2 (not divisible by 3)
- 17 % 4 = 1 (not divisible by 4)
- No divisors found

Conclusion: 17 is only divisible by 1 and 17 itself.

FINAL ANSWER: YES
