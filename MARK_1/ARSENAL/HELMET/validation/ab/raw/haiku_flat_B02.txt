- Memory safety vs. performance
- Expressiveness vs. simplicity
- Ecosystem maturity vs. modernity
- Development speed vs. runtime efficiency

1. The specific problem being solved
2. Team expertise and preferences
3. Performance requirements
4. Available libraries and frameworks
5. Deployment constraints
6. Maintenance and scalability needs
