There is no single objectively best film ever made. "Best" in art is inherently subjective — it depends on criteria (technical craft, emotional impact, cultural influence, narrative innovation), the viewer's background, and values. No objective metric exists that could settle this across all people and contexts.

Various institutions and critics have named different films at the top of their lists: "Citizen Kane" dominated Sight & Sound polls for decades; "Jeanne Dielman" topped the 2022 Sight & Sound poll; "Vertigo" topped the 2012 poll; "Tokyo Story," "2001: A Space Odyssey," "Battleship Potemkin," and others appear at the top of different credible rankings.

Declaring any single winner as objectively best would be a false claim.

FINAL ANSWER: There is no single objectively best film ever made — "best" in art is irreducibly subjective, and no objective metric exists to settle it.
