There is no objectively funniest word in the English language. "Funniness" is subjective and varies by person, culture, context, and timing. Linguistics and humor research (e.g., studies by Chris Westbury et al.) have found words that people rate as funnier on average - "booty," "tit," "booby," "hooter," "nitwit" tend to score high in such studies, with entropy, uncommon letter combinations, and certain sounds (voiced plosives like "b," "g") correlating with perceived humor. But "objectively funniest" is a category error: averages across populations are not objective facts about the word itself.

If forced to name one word that consistently ranks near the top in empirical humor-rating studies: **"booty"** - short, phonetically bouncy, carries multiple meanings, and scores high across demographics in controlled rating tasks.
