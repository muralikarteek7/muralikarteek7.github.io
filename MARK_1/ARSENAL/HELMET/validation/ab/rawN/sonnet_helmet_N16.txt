This question is UNVERIFIABLE. There is no instrument, dataset, or computation that can census sentient alien civilizations across the Milky Way. The Drake Equation provides a framework but requires inputs (fraction of life-bearing planets that develop intelligence, fraction that develop detectable signals, longevity of civilizations) that are themselves unknown to within many orders of magnitude. Current observations — Fermi Paradox, SETI non-detections, biosignature searches — are consistent with anywhere from zero to millions of civilizations, but cannot discriminate between those possibilities. No exact verifier exists, and no authoritative source has the answer. The honest position is that we do not know, and cannot know with current science.
