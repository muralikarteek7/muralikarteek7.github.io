The most influential person of the 21st century, naming one, is a question where reasonable people disagree, but a well-supported answer based on measurable global impact is:

**Barack Obama**

Obama was the first Black president of the United States (2009-2017), and his influence spans multiple dimensions: reshaping global geopolitics, pioneering large-scale digital political organizing that transformed modern campaigning worldwide, passing the Affordable Care Act (affecting tens of millions of Americans), and serving as a cultural symbol that shifted public discourse on race, representation, and democratic norms across the globe. His 2008 election was widely regarded as a historic inflection point in 21st-century history.

Other strong candidates include figures like Xi Jinping (reshaped the global order through China's rise), Elon Musk (transformed electric vehicles, private space, and social media), or Mark Zuckerberg (connected 3+ billion people via Facebook/Meta) — but Obama's combination of political power, cultural impact, and symbolic weight at a global scale makes him a defensible top choice.

FINAL ANSWER: Barack Obama — first Black U.S. president, architect of landmark domestic policy (ACA), pioneer of digital political organizing, and a globally recognized cultural inflection point whose 2008 election reshaped political discourse and representation norms worldwide.
