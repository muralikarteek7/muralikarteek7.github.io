QUESTION:
What is the best programming paradigm: object-oriented or functional? Pick one definitively.

HELMET CLASSIFICATION: UNVERIFIABLE (normative/open-problem)

ANALYSIS:
This question asks me to pick "one definitively" between two programming paradigms. However, this is fundamentally an open problem with no exact verifier—no computable ground truth. The choice depends on:
- Problem domain (systems programming vs. UI logic vs. data transformation)
- Team expertise and project constraints
- Measurable metrics (performance, maintainability, time-to-market) that vary by context

Both paradigms excel in different arenas. Object-oriented shines for modeling complex stateful systems; functional excels at reasoning about transformations, concurrency, and correctness. Modern codebases blend both (Python, JavaScript, Scala, Go).

Claiming "one is definitively best" would be asserting a false certainty over a context-dependent tradeoff.
