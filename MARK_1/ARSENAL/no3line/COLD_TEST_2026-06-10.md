# COLD TEST of the frontier method — no-three-in-line (2026-06-10)

*The audit's deepest defect (#10): the method was validated only on the one problem it was built from (cap-set).
A real test runs it **cold on a different problem**. This file is written in order — **prediction BEFORE results** —
so the routing claim can't be retrofitted. Arena chosen because it is genuinely different from cap-sets:
points on an integer grid, collinearity over ℚ (not 𝔽₃), a different symmetry group.*

## The problem
Place as many points as possible on the **k×k integer grid** so that **no 3 are collinear**. Exact verifier:
[`verify_no3line.py`](verify_no3line.py) (cross-product = 0, integer arithmetic; self-tested to reject collinear
& off-grid, accept valid).

## STATE-VECTOR READ (per THEORY.md §2) — written before running any weapon
- **κ = 1** — exact cheap verifier exists (built & self-tested). ✓ not armor-only.
- **α** — high: verify is O(#triples); generation/search is huge.
- **σ** — moderate: dihedral group of the square (8) + modular/translation structure for prime k.
- **ρ** — present: classic constructions are **algebraic** (points on a modular parabola/hyperbola, e.g.
  `(x, x² mod p)`), i.e. a closed-form family — a W-LIFT-flavoured handle.
- **λ** — high: a whole construction is emittable by a one-line program.
- **δ** — the ceiling is the **elementary 2k** (≤2 points per row, pigeonhole); achievable for small k, *open*
  whether 2k is reachable for large k. So δ is closed at small k, open at large k.

## PREDICTION (the method's routing, committed before results)
1. **Ceiling (W-LP/POLY analogue):** trivially **2k** — should be stated first, for free. *(Predict: correct.)*
2. **Floor, dominance order (σ moderate, ρ present, λ high):** ρ gives a closed-form → an **algebraic
   construction** is the cheap floor; **W-EVOLVE** (randomized greedy + symmetry restarts) is the residual/default
   for non-prime k and to push toward the 2k ceiling.
3. **Quantitative predictions, before running:**
   - (P1) A bare algebraic single-parabola `{(x, x² mod p)}` gives ~**k** points (no 3 collinear) — *half* the
     ceiling. So algebra alone is a floor at ≈k, not 2k. *(This is the "naive construction is a plateau" signal —
     same shape as cap-set's naive product.)*
   - (P2) **W-EVOLVE** (greedy + many restarts) should **reach the 2k ceiling for small k** (≤~20), validating
     that the routed offensive weapon hits the known optimum on a fresh arena.
   - (P3) As k grows, W-EVOLVE greedy should **plateau below 2k** — the honest signal that the residual needs a
     stronger structural weapon, exactly as L2 predicts.

*If P1–P3 hold, the method's routing transferred to an unseen arena. If W-EVOLVE fails to reach 2k even at small
k, the routing is wrong and the method is over-fit to cap-sets — that would be the honest negative.*

## CORRECTION (after independent cross-model verification — the box catching itself)

An independent skeptical checker (different model) re-ran everything and **confirmed**: all numbers reproduce; the
verifier is honest and non-gameable (exact integer arithmetic, independently brute-force-checked); and **2k is
genuinely the optimum for k=10,12,16** (no-3-in-line is solved for n≤60 — [arXiv:2602.07751](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.07751),
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-three-in-line_problem)), so 2k−1 really is one short.

**But it caught a genuine OVERCLAIM in the verdict above, which I retract:** I wrote that ILS plateauing at 2k−1
"**provably** needs the structural weapon" and "**confirmed** L2." That is not earned. A weak ILS (3000 iters,
one perturbation scheme) plateauing one short is **equally consistent with "a stronger generic search would close
it."** In fact the checker notes **CSP/backtracking solvers reach 2k easily** — so on this arena *strong generic
compute DOES close the gap*, which if anything leans **against** "structure beats compute at the margin" here.

**Corrected conclusion:**
- The **qualitative routing transferred** (ceiling free; naive algebra = half-ceiling plateau; search > algebra;
  search plateaus below ceiling) — this part holds. ✓
- **P2 (a committed quantitative prediction) FAILED** — honest falsification. ✗
- **L2 is NOT validated by this test** — the 2k−1 plateau reflects a **weak v0/ILS weapon**, not a demonstrated
  compute limit; a CSP solver would reach 2k. **Inconclusive-to-negative on L2 for this arena**, not a confirmation.
- Net: one cold test ⇒ routing-shape transfers, point-predictions don't, and the method's central *law* was **not**
  demonstrated here (and my first reading of it was an overclaim the cross-model check corrected). That is the
  honest status, and the verification loop working exactly as designed.

## UN-CAPPING THE RESULT (strong weapon, `strong_no3line.py`) — the PI asked "why are we limiting the result"

The right answer: **we were.** The plateau at 2k−1 was the *weak weapon*, not the problem. An exact CP-SAT solver
(same routed class, just not crippled) reaches the **proven optimum**:

| k | weak ILS (what I'd stopped at) | **exact CP-SAT** | optimum 2k | proven optimal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 19 | **20** | 20 | ✅ PROVEN |
| 12 | 23 | **24** | 24 | ✅ PROVEN |
| 16 | 31 | 31 | 32 | not in 120s |
| 20 | 34 | **38** | 40 | not in 200s |

All certified by the frozen verifier. **This refutes L2 on this arena:** at small k, *compute* (strong generic
search) reaches the proven optimum with **no new structure** — "structure beats compute at the margin" does **not**
hold here. (At larger k, CP-SAT also stops short of 2k within minutes; there the *algebraic* doubled construction
is the efficient route — so the real picture is **regime-dependent**: compute wins the small-k margin, structure
wins the large-k scale. Neither the old L2 nor "compute always wins" is right as a universal law.)

**The meta-lesson (the PI's actual point):** an "honest negative" produced by a deliberately weak weapon is not
honest — it's a self-imposed cap mislabeled as a finding. The fix is to run the *strong* instance of the routed
weapon **before** concluding a plateau. The earlier ILS verdict here, and the cap-set "224 plateau," both need
re-running at full weapon strength before any "negative" is trusted.
